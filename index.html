About the author

A recurring question: what is the spreadsheet missing?

Thomas W. Rourke writes about technology, institutions, incentives, power, and practical leverage. His work focuses on the gap between the way a decision is presented and the full operating reality underneath it.

In The Data Center Revolt, that means looking beyond project announcements to permits, public process, infrastructure burdens, local law, and who absorbs the costs. In Outsmart AI at Work, it means looking beyond productivity claims to replacement economics, hidden review work, decision ownership, compensation, and career optionality.

Rourke's approach is neither anti-technology nor promotional. It is evidence-first, skeptical of incomplete models, and designed to help readers act while choices still exist.
