The framework

A defense system, not a panic checklist.

The seven walls organize the evidence that matters: money at risk, early warning, replacement economics, decision rights, sponsorship, compensation, and the way you use AI without teaching the organization to value only your cheapest visible output.

Wall 1

Protect the Money

Know the complete compensation and career exposure at risk before a role, title, or pay change occurs.

Wall 2

See the Attack Coming

Read patterns in documentation, metrics, authority, planning access, and budget language before the story becomes official.

Wall 3

Make Replacement Expensive

Price vacancy, transition, supervision, exceptions, review, rework, and the cost of rebuilding operating capacity.

Wall 4

Own the Decisions

Move the center of your role from production to recommendation, approval, rejection, escalation, and consequence ownership.

Wall 5

Build Political Protection

Create several legitimate internal reasons for leaders to preserve your authority, capability, and role.

Wall 6

Defend the Pay

Negotiate salary, bonus, equity, title, benefits, authority, severance, and timing as one economic system.

Wall 7

Stay Current

Use AI competently, verify it, document what remains human, and avoid proving only that your visible output is cheaper.
