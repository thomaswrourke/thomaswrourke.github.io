Threat detection

Threat Signal Scorecard

Score facts, not suspicions. A score does not predict a cut. It tells you when preparation becomes rational.

1. Process documentation

Is the request becoming a time-and-transfer study?

2. Consultant activity

Cost, volume, headcount, spans, layers, location?

3. Unit-output metrics

Are outcomes being replaced by units, hours, tickets, reports?

4. Relationships move

Are clients, vendors, or decision-makers moving away from your role?

5. One-way backup training

Are you transferring knowledge without a future-scope discussion?

6. Decision rights shrink

Are approvals or authority moving elsewhere?

7. Role language weakens

Owning and deciding become supporting and coordinating?

8. Unsupported targets

Do aggressive targets arrive without capacity or assumptions?

9. Planning exclusion

Are you in delivery but no longer in design or budget discussions?

10. New overlapping role

Is a new role absorbing your strategic or decision work?

11. Budget language changes

Run-rate, structural cost, location strategy, zero-based review?

12. Future investment stops

Development and long-term assignments disappear?

One-Page Compensation Brief

Copy-ready prompts

Use AI without outsourcing judgment

1. Replacement Economics Stress Test

You are helping me stress-test a proposed automation, outsourcing, consolidation, or lower-cost replacement plan. Do not assume the plan will fail and do not assume it will succeed. Separate confirmed facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns. Identify: (1) visible labor removed, (2) transition cost, (3) review and supervision created, (4) exception handling, (5) rework and correction, (6) cross-functional labor shifted outside the project budget, (7) client, compliance, timing, and reputation consequences, (8) break-even assumptions, and (9) the business evidence needed to validate each assumption. End with the five most important questions management should answer before claiming a net saving.

2. Task-to-Decision Conversion

I will give you a list of activities from my role. For each activity, help me distinguish the physical task from the business use, decision, consequence, authority, and evidence. Do not inflate my contribution or assign authority I do not actually have. Flag any statement that sounds like unsupported self-promotion. Then rewrite each activity as a concise, truthful ownership statement that would survive skeptical review by finance, HR, or a senior operator.

3. Compensation Evidence Auditor

Audit my compensation case as if you were a skeptical compensation committee. Separate direct evidence, operational evidence, corroborated estimates, and personal estimates. Identify overclaiming, double-counting, vague language, missing baselines, weak attribution, and unsupported market comparisons. Then produce a one-page brief with: material scope change, three to five strongest outcomes, decision and risk ownership, alignment evidence, exact request, and likely objections. Do not invent any fact or market number.

4. Threat Pattern Analyst

I will describe recent workplace events. Build two columns: fact and interpretation. For every event, provide at least one innocent explanation and one plausible threat explanation. Look for patterns involving transferability, comparability, authority, planning access, succession, budget language, and documentation. Do not tell me that a layoff is certain. Tell me what additional evidence would materially change the probability and what low-regret preparation would be useful even if the threat interpretation is wrong.

5. AI Failure and Control Review

Review this AI-assisted workflow as a control system, not as a product demo. Identify what the tool produces, what inputs it depends on, what can be plausibly wrong, what requires human verification, which exceptions need escalation, who should own release, and what evidence should be logged. Distinguish normal transition issues from structural control requirements. Propose the smallest practical set of controls that preserves the benefit without pretending the output is self-validating.

6. Negotiation Script Builder

Help me prepare a calm compensation or role-scope conversation. I will provide the facts, evidence, exact request, fallback terms, and timing. Build a short script using four steps: acknowledge, state the business issue, make the request, define the next step. Then give me three likely responses from management and one concise reply to each. Do not use threats, emotional language, bluffing, or legal claims.

7. Executive Value Narrative

Turn the material I provide into an executive value narrative. Structure it as: what changed; what the old metric stopped showing; the complete result; the decision required; what happens if no decision is made. Keep it evidence-first and under 180 words. Preserve uncertainty where it exists. Do not replace numbers with adjectives such as strategic, critical, transformational, or significant unless the evidence supports them.

8. 90-Day Role Defense Review

Act as a rigorous quarterly reviewer of my career position. Using only the information I provide, assess seven areas: money at risk, threat signals, replacement economics, decision ownership, sponsor coverage, compensation readiness, and AI relevance. For each area, identify one strength, one weakness, one missing piece of evidence, and one action for the next 30 days. End with the three highest-leverage actions for the quarter. Do not invent company facts or predict employment outcomes.
Installation plan

90 days to install the system

Days 1-30

Price and observe

□ Calculate current total compensation and one Career Exposure scenario.
□ Build the Threat Signal Scorecard using facts from the last 90 days.
□ Start an Exception Ledger and Decision Journal.
□ Map the three decisions in your role with the greatest consequence.
Days 31-60

Make value legible

□ Build the first Hidden Replacement Bill using restrained assumptions.
□ Convert five task statements into decision-ownership statements.
□ Identify two sponsor candidates and send one useful sponsor update.
□ Build the first one-page value narrative before a real decision meeting.
Days 61-90

Align and preserve options

□ Build the Compensation Evidence File and exact package request.
□ Confirm the compensation / budget / talent decision calendar.
□ Test one approved AI workflow and document review, rejection, and human ownership.
□ Refresh external market information and preserve at least one credible alternative.
Quarterly

Review the fortress

□ What changed in my economic exposure?
□ Which warning signals strengthened or weakened?
□ Which decisions do I now own?
□ Which sponsor actually acted?
□ What productivity gain became a new expectation?
□ What evidence will matter before the next decision window?
The toolkit is intentionally incomplete without your judgment. Its purpose is to make assumptions, decisions, and evidence visible. It should not produce a false sense of certainty.
