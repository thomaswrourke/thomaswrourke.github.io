Copy-ready prompts

Use AI without outsourcing judgment.

These prompts are designed to expose assumptions and structure evidence. Remove confidential information before using any external AI service.

Prompt 1 / Threat signal audit
Act as a skeptical operating analyst. I will give you factual changes I have observed at work. Separate them into: confirmed facts, plausible interpretations, unsupported assumptions, and information still missing. Do not predict layoffs. Then suggest the three cheapest pieces of evidence I could gather next without violating policy or confidentiality.
Prompt 2 / Replacement bill
Help me build a conservative replacement-cost model for a role. Separate recurring future cost from one-time transition cost. Consider vacancy coverage, search, onboarding, supervision, exception handling, review, rework, delayed decisions, client continuity, compliance, and time to competence. Mark every assumption and do not invent numbers.
Prompt 3 / Decision rights
Given this workflow description, identify each material decision, who recommends, who approves, who can reject, what threshold triggers escalation, and who owns the consequence if the decision is wrong. Distinguish production work from decision rights. Ask for missing facts before concluding.
Prompt 4 / Exception ledger
Turn these operating incidents into a neutral exception ledger. For each event, capture the standard path, what failed, the human judgment or relationship used, time required, consequence avoided or created, and whether the exception is likely to recur. Do not exaggerate causality.
Prompt 5 / Sponsor map
Help me distinguish friends, allies, operational partners, sponsor candidates, and active sponsors. For each person I describe, identify the business result they own, how my work affects it, their decision access, and the evidence that they would actually advocate for my scope or role. Do not confuse friendliness with sponsorship.
Prompt 6 / Compensation evidence
Help me turn these facts into a one-page compensation brief. Organize: current scope, material scope change, business results, decision/risk ownership, internal or market alignment evidence, and exact requested alignment. Use calm business language. Remove emotional claims that are not supported by evidence.
Prompt 7 / AI value capture
Analyze this AI-assisted workflow. Compare old time, generation time, review/correction time, rejected outputs, exception rate, human decision points, and business result. Identify what became cheaper and what human responsibility remained or increased. Do not equate faster drafting with full job replacement.
Prompt 8 / 90-day installation
Using the 7-Wall framework, build a 90-day operating plan from the facts I provide. Prioritize no more than three actions per 30-day block. Each action needs: evidence to gather, conversation to have, artifact to produce, risk to avoid, and a clear completion condition. Prefer reversible moves before irreversible ones.
Do not paste trade secrets, personal employee data, regulated information, or other confidential material into an AI system unless your organization has explicitly approved that use.
Installation plan

90-Day Operating Plan

The goal is not to become "indispensable" in a theatrical way. It is to improve evidence, authority, sponsorship, compensation alignment, AI fluency, and external optionality in a sequence you can actually execute.

30

Days 1-30: See and price

60

Days 31-60: Move toward decisions

90

Days 61-90: Align pay and options

The rule

If the evidence says the role is genuinely shrinking, do not spend three months proving a story you want to be true. Use the plan to improve your timing and options.

Review after 90 days. Keep what produced evidence or options. Drop rituals that only made you feel busy.
